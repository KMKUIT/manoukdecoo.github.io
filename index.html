Decorative painter with a background in murals at different locations in the Netherlands. Skilled in restoring and embellishing historical and contemporary surfaces with an eye for detail and artistic precision. Eager to learn and develop various traditional art forms.

About Me

Hi! I am Manouk de Coo, 19 years old and I live in Utrecht. I have been studying to become a specialist in restorative and decorative painting since 2023. I am doing this at the Nimeto vocational college in Utrecht. Alongside my studies, I am exploring as many different art styles as possible. You can follow my creative development on this webiste.
